Six Micro-Tests Score App

Students answer six very short tests. Each test contains five quick questions on a 1–5 scale. At the end, the app gives one overall score for each of the six items, ready to copy into a dataframe for the next step.

Optional interpretation

The goal is not to rank students as better or worse, but to create a profile vector that can be used later for clustering, visualisation, or comparison.
  • Speed: quick vs careful decision-making
  • Risk: cautious vs risk-seeking responses
  • Structure: preference for clear instructions and rules
  • Ambiguity: comfort with unclear situations
  • Social: independent vs collaborative style
  • Pattern: rule-based vs intuitive thinking
