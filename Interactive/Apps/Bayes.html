The Stolen Laptop

You are the judge in a Bayesian courtroom. Minh is on trial for stealing a laptop from a university study room.

Before hearing any evidence, you start with a flat prior:

Beta(1, 1)

Across eight rounds, the prosecution and defence take turns presenting evidence. Your job is to rate how strongly each piece of evidence points toward guilt.

  • Move the slider from 0 to 10.
  • 0 means the evidence strongly favours innocence.
  • 5 means the evidence is neutral.
  • 10 means the evidence strongly favours guilt.

The app treats your rating as binomial-style evidence and updates the posterior distribution. The posterior then becomes the prior for the next round.
