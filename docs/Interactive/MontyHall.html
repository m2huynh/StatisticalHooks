MontyHall

Probability Conditional Probability Simulation Bayesian Reasoning

About

The Monty Hall problem is one of the most famous (and most argued-about) puzzles in probability. Students are almost always confident that staying or switching makes no difference — after all, with two doors left, isn’t it 50/50? This interactive app lets students test that intuition directly: they play the game themselves, repeatedly, and watch a live crosstab build up showing how often “stay” wins versus how often “switch” wins. The gap between intuition and the empirical result is what makes this activity land — no lecture is as convincing as students’ own accumulating data.
